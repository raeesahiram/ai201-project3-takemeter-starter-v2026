# Milestone 1 — community and candidate distinctions

## Community

r/LivestreamFail — https://www.reddit.com/r/LivestreamFail/

Public, readable without an account, and not built around one show or release.
Posts are mostly clip links with a one-line title, so the text worth labelling
is in the top-level comments.

## Candidate distinctions

1. **Some comments name something checkable. Most don't.** A timestamp, a
   viewer or sub count, a date, a past stream or an earlier clip — versus no
   evidence at all.
2. **Some comments add context the clip leaves out.** What happened before the
   clip started, what was said the day before, history between two streamers —
   versus responding only to the seconds that are shown.
3. **Some comments judge the person; others describe what happened.** "He's
   always been a fraud" versus "He muted the mic right before reading the
   donation."
4. **Some comments are just a reaction.** Short, dependent on the clip, no claim
   you could agree or disagree with: "LMAO," "bro is cooked," emotes, copypasta.
